## Question and scope

Turn the information-limit result into a conservative, testable adoption
procedure. Subtract shared semantic-coverage packets before bounding the paired
cost difference; derive a support-valid range for complete bounded-width epochs.
Combine a one-sided finite-catalogue Hoeffding bound with a declared total
variation shift radius and installation amortization. Prove each step and
attribute the concentration/risk-testing ingredients to prior work. The new
claim is the HACT-specific cost cancellation and conditional adoption contract,
not a new concentration inequality or general distribution-shift solution.

The catalogue, incumbent, packet contract, maximum wave width, exact wave count,
confidence level, shift radius and installation horizon are fixed before
calibration. Calibration episodes must be independent of fitting and IID across
episodes. Within-episode dependencies are unrestricted. All future laws must
remain on the same declared support and within the asserted TV radius; the
procedure cannot estimate that radius or independence from repeated traces.
It reports a conditional expected padded-byte benefit, never task acceptance,
realized speed, CPU benefit or automatic deployment permission.

## Validation and measurements

1. Exhaustively enumerate bounded-width complete partitions on up to six checks
   and evaluate literal labeled trees independently of production cost helpers.
   Check cancellation, support envelopes, root exactness and differing orders.
2. Check certificate arithmetic with high precision and one-sided tail calculations;
   reject malformed, incomplete, duplicated or out-of-support completions and
   mismatched registry/packet contracts. Missing sampling/shift evidence yields
   UNKNOWN, not statistical certification.
3. Run a labeled synthetic calibration study on the exact complete-epoch witness.
   Both laws and layouts are fixed before sampling; all seeds and outcomes stay.
   Compare cancellation-aware versus whole-cost bounds, including negative laws,
   insufficient samples, shift sensitivity and installation costs. This is an
   analytical/synthetic validation, not a software productivity experiment.
4. Recompute descriptive range/sample-size diagnostics on archived public data.
   Do not relabel repeated snapshots or controlled dispatch shifts as IID natural
   calibration. Any such row stays UNCERTIFIED despite a positive sample mean.
5. Run focused and full prototype checks and independent historical audits,
   compile manuscript variants, inspect PDF rendering, and preserve provenance.

No v5-v12 observations or experiment protocols may be edited. New results live
under results/v13. CPU only; no model APIs, new external test campaigns, paid

Keep top-journal significance separate from numerical/software validation.
