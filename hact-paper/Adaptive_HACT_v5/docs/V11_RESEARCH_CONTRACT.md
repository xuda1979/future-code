## Estimands and controls

Primary representation comparison: total strict-epoch padded certificate bytes
on runs 3-4, including initial construction, bounded full-wave selection versus
the historical pairwise-order compact method. Max refined subtree size is 32;
64 is an explicitly secondary sensitivity. Report original compact, pairwise
compact and their same-order refined variants, plus incumbent-only selection.
Same-order topology gains do not isolate the informational value of higher
moments; that distinction must remain explicit.

Compile/selection wall time, manifest bytes and serialized packet bytes are
separate endpoints. Report lifecycle break-even including one compilation and
installation. Do not add storage bytes to wire bytes. A common deterministic
answer codec forces equal answer bytes; this is a correctness control.

For service comparison freeze a layout-independent consumer request schedule
before timing: initial, intermediate and final status/coverage/failure/replay
requests, cold and repeated warm queries. This is a controlled integration
workload, not observed production-user demand. Compare the maintained HACT
service to both the existing indexed ledger and a ledger with maintained
status counters. Charge setup, every ingest and query, encoding and decoding;
checker execution is reported separately. Repeated paired runs randomize arm
order with fixed seeds and are timing repeats, not new project replication.
No live-agent productivity, physical-WAN or provider-token result is claimed.
