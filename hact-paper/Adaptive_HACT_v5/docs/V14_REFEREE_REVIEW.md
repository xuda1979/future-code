# HACT v14: scientific review and revision response

2026-10-22. AI-assisted internal review, not independent human peer review.
This revision targets review as a theory-led software-engineering paper with
reproducible systems boundaries. Editorial acceptance remains a journal decision.

## Central argument

The manuscript answers an identifiable question: which completion information
is sufficient for the declared packet objective, and when can an advisory
representation change without changing semantic acceptance? The general
pairwise-information obstruction is complemented by a constructive null regime:
under exchangeable complete batches, cardinality alone yields a global optimum.
The empirical study tests these modeling boundaries rather than claiming
general superiority for a tree service that the evidence does not support.

## Proof review

For each coverage cardinality s, each declared batch of size w is a uniform
w-subset of the full n-check registry. Its miss probability is
C(n-s,w)/C(n,w). Linearity of expectation needs no independence among batches.
The cold charge adds one to every internal node's weight. Subtree states must
always use this full-registry weight, not recompute a law on the local subtree.
Conditioning on root arity and positive child sizes gives the forest recurrence;
induction covers every feasible shape. Exchangeability removes label dependence.
Multiplying by a common denominator preserves exact comparisons and ties.
If the future epoch retains the exchangeable law conditional on training
and the selected layout, conditioning on that fixed tree and then averaging
shows that learning cannot beat the global optimum. Independent training
and future epochs suffice. Unconditional exchangeability alone does not
exclude predictive leakage from training into a future completion pattern.

The semantic proof still assumes trusted immutable checking, exact identities
and atomic local generation fencing. No advisory optimizer or positive byte
estimate grants a publication pass. The adoption theorem still requires external
IID-calibration and same-support workload-shift evidence; public rows remain
UNKNOWN where that evidence is absent.

## Submission judgment

The completed revision is a concrete candidate for author and external expert
review on its information-theoretic result, model-specific tractability result,
semantic contract and candid empirical boundary study. Natural hierarchical
consumer utility remains an open empirical question, not a claimed achievement.
A stronger general service-benefit paper would require a new prospectively
sampled consumer study; inventing or relabeling one would undermine this paper.
IEEE TSE remains the ambitious target; the authors should assess theoretical
novelty, venue fit and final factual declarations before journal submission.
Publishing the requested PR does not submit the paper to a journal.

`submission/TSE_PREPARATION.md` and `submission/TSE_UPLOAD_MAP.md`; the primary
