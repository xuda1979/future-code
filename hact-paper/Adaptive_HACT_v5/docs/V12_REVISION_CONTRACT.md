# HACT v12 referee revision contract

Recorded 2026-10-21 before new v12 computations. Parent is local v11 commit
`e67afe9c3950b9b1d3691a3c62a066893cb9ac1f`. This is an internal revision plan,
not a preregistration or an independent human review.

## Objective and acceptance

Review v11 skeptically, correct scientific and presentation weaknesses, and
prepare a journal submission package for the authors' review. Prefer a journal
whose scope matches the supported contribution; do not equate passing software
tests with journal significance or acceptance. No journal submission is made.

The central review risks are: the connection between the information theorem
and the fixed-eight completion policy; insufficient distinction between novel
results and established ingredients; unclear interpretation of repeated-suite
sampling and timing repeats; and incomplete venue-specific preparation.

1. Check both existing proofs and their information interfaces independently.
2. Test whether uniform filler allocation lifts matching pairwise statistics
   to equal-width complete epochs. If valid, prove the exact supported claim
   about two feasible local layout choices. Do not promote it to a global-tree
   optimum claim without a proof.
3. Review all empirical claims from immutable v11 observations. Any new timing
   uncertainty or sensitivity summaries are retrospective descriptive analysis,
   not new experiments or confirmatory tests. Preserve all exclusions and losses.
4. Rewrite the journal manuscript around the strongest supported contribution,
   with quantitative abstract, clear research questions, limitations and novelty
   comparisons. Prepare the selected venue's required manuscript variants and
   submission documents using current primary guidance where available.
5. Validate new numerical logic with independent arithmetic and corruption
   checks; rebuild and visually inspect changed PDFs. Refresh a release manifest
   and save a complete, reviewable package.

## Constraints and authority

- Keep v5-v11 raw observations and analysis receipts immutable.
- Keep the approved acceptance registry and runtime semantics unchanged.
- CPU-only, no paid model APIs or external services, no accelerator work.
- New work budget: 90 minutes local wall time and 300 MiB retained new artifacts.
- Root owns manuscript, venue preparation and final integration. A narrow
  theory verifier may own new analytical reference/tests; an empirical referee
  may own its review and descriptive robustness analysis. No overlapping writers.
- The preceding GitHub push was rejected by automatic approval review. Do not
  retry or use an alternative publication path without explicit authorization.
- Do not attest author consent, institutional approval, funding, conflicts,
  exclusivity, copyright, or publication fees. Draft documents may identify the
  remaining factual author inputs without inventing them.

## Stop rules

Reject an analytical extension if its algebra, information equality or scoring
scope fails an independent check. Preserve any counterexample. Do not select a
more favorable empirical endpoint after seeing outcomes. Complete the supported
revision and explicitly report any substantive evidence gap that remains.
