\section{Implications and Threats to Validity}\label{sec:threats}
\paragraph{Operational implication.}
An indexed ledger is the appropriate initial baseline for the declared
status, coverage and diagnostic queries. The corrected study's common codec
precludes a wire-byte advantage at that service. If a deployment independently
requires the hierarchical packet format, learned ordering can reduce its
within-format cost on structured completion traces. This conditional saving
does not establish the value of maintaining a hierarchy in the first place.
Exact fitting remains an offline reference, and a frozen incumbent remains a
valid deployment choice.

\paragraph{Construct and internal validity.}
The primary optimization target is an additive padded-packet model. Canonical
bytes, compressed bytes, page counts, serialized state, RAM, model tokens and
latency are distinct metrics. The corrected study separates those it measures,
and its arrival model is explicitly conditional. Common checker time can mask
post-query responsiveness, so the reported Amdahl fraction is not a bound on
all diagnostic workloads. Query caches are exercised, but one local timing
campaign provides no statistical deployment-speedup estimate. Older scripts
and results are versioned historical records; corrected studies do not turn
archived evidence into independent samples. The newly generated result prose
addresses sign, unit and subset errors that the earlier consistency audit missed.

\paragraph{External validity.}
The source cohort contains twelve archived candidates from NetworkX, Toolz
and Future Code. Synthetic cells have three seeds and fixed generators;
source cases are not an independently selected natural-bug or demand corpus.
Repeated layouts and cache requests do not increase the number of independent
candidates. No new hosted model, physical-WAN or provider-token measurement is
claimed. The paired query study is a retrospective implementation comparison,
not a live long-horizon agent evaluation. New repositories and time periods,
prospectively sampled query demand and repeated host measurements are needed
to establish an applicability region beyond the packet-format results.

\paragraph{Security and semantic boundary.}
Theorem~\ref{thm:safety} assumes a complete approved predicate set, correctly
bound dependencies, a trusted checker, collision-resistant binding and atomic
local fencing. HACT provides no checker attestation, distributed consensus,
non-repudiation or OS sandbox. Its local capsules are not authenticated
membership proofs. A merge or deployment service must separately bind the
verified candidate to the integration head atomically. Layout independence
preserves a declared contract; it cannot establish the contract's adequacy.

\paragraph{Research priorities.}
The next study should compare independently implemented services under
prospectively sampled diagnostic requests, including state changes, cache
misses and migration, on held-out repositories. It should test whether any
measured benefit repays compilation and maintenance under an explicit
critical-path model. Live-agent and multi-host evaluation can follow when
such a service advantage is established; their outcomes remain unknown.

\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
Adaptive HACT supplies a conditional local mechanism for changing evidence
layouts while preserving the acceptance contract. Completion sets define its
packet-refresh objective. Identical pairwise statistics can require different
globally optimal trees under this model, while the demonstrated practical
algorithm uses pairwise seriation and independent validation. Frozen experiments support within-format
byte and exhaustive-page reductions when completion locality is structured.
The corrected service comparison gives equal answer bytes as a consequence
of the common codec and records additional tree-state costs. It cannot
establish a bandwidth advantage. Comparative diagnostic utility and
agent-productivity benefits remain unestablished. The supported design lesson is precise: representation
may adapt, while semantic identities and the evidence authorizing progress
remain protected.
