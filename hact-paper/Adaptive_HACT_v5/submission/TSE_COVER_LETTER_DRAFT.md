# Unsent cover letter draft: IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering

Dear Editors,

Please consider “Adaptive HACT: Information Limits and Safe Adaptation of
Verification Evidence” by Da Xu and Fei Wang, China Mobile Research Institute.

The paper studies how verification evidence may be reorganized while preserving
its acceptance contract. It proves that exact pairwise completion information
can leave globally optimal evidence trees unidentified, including a sharp
minimax penalty and a fixed-batching candidate-class extension. A local
publication invariant separates layout adaptation from semantic authority.
Under exchangeable complete batches, an exact global size-state optimizer
provides a complementary no-learning baseline. We compare this specialization
explicitly with established batch-update group-key tree optimization.

The revision also characterizes exact population scoring interfaces and gives
a conditional adoption rule based on cancelled paired packet costs, independent
calibration, an externally justified same-support workload-shift bound and
installation amortization. We distinguish these specializations from standard
linear identification, concentration and multiple-testing methods.

The empirical contribution is deliberately bounded. The artifact retains weak
or negative refinement/transfer results, resource exclusions and stronger
maintained-counter ledger controls. It does not claim a general service or
agent speedup. Exact rational references, independent literal scoring,
corruption tests, versioned observations and complete source are supplied.
The exact symmetric baseline wins on the item-shuffled dispatch traces and
loses on the file-group traces and original repeated-order holdouts, defining
a concrete boundary for exploiting learned completion locality.

The authors will finalize all factual submission declarations, publication
history, contributions, funding, conflicts and permissions before using this
letter. This draft makes none of those attestations.
