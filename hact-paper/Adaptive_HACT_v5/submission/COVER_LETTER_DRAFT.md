# Unsent cover letter for final author review

Dear Editors of the Journal of Systems and Software,

We would like to submit “Adaptive HACT: Information Limits and Safe Adaptation
of Verification Evidence,” by Da Xu and Fei Wang of China Mobile Research
Institute, for consideration as a regular research article.

The paper studies how verification evidence can be reorganized without changing
the acceptance contract that it authorizes. Its central result is an explicit
information limit: exact pairwise completion probabilities can leave globally
optimal certificate trees unidentified over a general family of affine packet
prices and fanouts. We derive sharp minimax penalties, prove a complete-epoch
separation, and connect the limitation to fixed-eight batching through an
equal-width padding argument with precisely delimited candidate-class scope.
A conditional publication invariant separates adaptive layouts from semantic
authority during migration.

The implementation supplies bounded full-wave refinement and independent
arithmetic and semantic checks. The empirical component evaluates the
boundaries of adoption. Five admitted public projects show small primary gains
on two repeated completion patterns and ties on three. Separate controlled
dispatch shifts retain three wins, eighteen ties and nine losses across thirty
admitted executions. A newly frozen service ablation removes byte-accounting
serialization; the tree remains slower than a maintained-counter ledger for
the declared diagnostic workload. The paper therefore distinguishes a
provable information limit and within-format savings from service superiority.

We believe this combination of formal results, verification infrastructure and
reproducible positive and negative evidence fits JSS's scope. The artifact
contains source, complete raw observations, source and environment manifests,
pre-execution protocols, exact references and independent numerical audits.
The manuscript states its trust assumptions, convenience sampling, provenance
repairs, resource exclusions and substantive AI assistance.

Correspondence may be directed to Da Xu at xudayj@chinamobile.com.
